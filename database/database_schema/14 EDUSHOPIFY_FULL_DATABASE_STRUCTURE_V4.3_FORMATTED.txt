1. CONFIRMED CORE BUSINESS RULES
=====================================================================

 1. A human logs in through users.
 2. Every marketplace user belongs to exactly one account.
 3. An account is either:
      - individual
      - organization
 4. One user cannot manage multiple marketplace accounts and cannot
    join another marketplace account.
 5. An organization may contain multiple users/members.
 6. An organization may have multiple owners.
 7. One account may have:
      - Buyer capability
      - Supplier capability
      - Both Buyer and Supplier capabilities
 8. Buyer and Supplier use separate profiles even when they belong
    to the same account.
 9. Every new Buyer or Supplier capability requires Admin approval.
10. Existing approved capability continues while a new capability
    application is pending.
11. An individual account may convert to an organization account.
12. Individual-to-organization conversion requires Admin approval.
13. During conversion review, the old individual identity remains
    active and the proposed organization data is not public.
14. If conversion is rejected, the account remains individual and
    the user may resubmit.
15. After conversion, rollback to individual is not allowed.
16. If an approved Supplier converts to an organization, Supplier
    capability becomes pending and requires Admin approval again.
17. Business records belong to accounts.
18. The acting human is recorded separately through fields such as
    created_by_user_id, submitted_by_user_id, and
    approved_by_user_id.
19. Spatie Laravel Permission Teams manages all live roles and
    permissions for Platform staff and account members.
20. account_id is configured as Spatie's team foreign key.
21. account_members remains the source of truth for business
    membership, owner status, joining, removal, and suspension.
22. Buyer and Supplier are account capabilities, not Spatie roles.
23. Standard roles may be global Spatie roles with
    roles.account_id = NULL.
24. Account-specific custom roles use roles.account_id = the
    current account id.
25. If an organization wants to customize a global standard role,
    the application clones it into an account-specific Spatie role
    and changes the clone.
26. Direct user permissions may be stored in model_has_permissions,
    but role-based permission assignment is preferred.
27. There is no custom permission-denial table. An ability is
    denied by default unless granted through a role or direct
    permission.
28. Supplier approval must happen before subscription activation.
29. A Supplier does not automatically receive a free plan. Admin
    may create a free plan, and the Supplier must select it.
30. A pending Supplier may create draft listings but cannot
    publish, see RFQs, or submit quotations.
31. Buyer and Supplier profiles intentionally store separate
    business/contact information.
32. Supplier documents are the same across countries and Supplier
    types; each document type is marked required or optional.
33. Supplier applications may be resubmitted only up to an
    Admin-configurable maximum.
34. Major Supplier legal-information changes set Supplier
    capability to revision_required, while current public listings
    remain visible unless Admin suspends them.
35. Supplier physical locations and service areas are separate.
36. Service areas support country, state, city, and later
    radius/API matching.
37. Buyer may send an RFQ:
      - globally
      - to one selected Supplier
      - to multiple selected Suppliers
38. Public RFQ summaries are searchable, but full details require
    backend eligibility.
39. RFQ visibility and notifications may be delayed by the
    Supplier subscription plan.
40. Directly selected Suppliers receive the RFQ immediately,
    subject to active Supplier capability and valid subscription.
41. One RFQ may contain multiple Products and Services from
    multiple categories.
42. Partial quotations are allowed.
43. Alternative quotation items are allowed.
44. Buyer may request formal quotation revisions and may also
    negotiate through messages.
45. Buyer may edit an RFQ after quotations exist; affected
    Suppliers are notified and may revise.
46. RFQ cancellation requires a reason.
47. Phase 1 supports one winning Supplier per RFQ.
48. Supplier must accept or reject an award within a deadline.
49. Supplier rejection requires a reason; Buyer may award another
    quotation.
50. After Supplier acceptance, the system creates/proceeds with a
    Purchase Order.
51. Product purchase payments are outside the platform in Phase 1.
52. Only Supplier subscription payments are processed inside the
    platform.
53. Platform revenue is subscription-based in Phase 1.
54. Buyer-Supplier disputes initially use the support-ticket
    system.
55. Reviews may be created after a Supplier submits a quotation.
    A later transaction review may also be supported.
56. Platform Admin/staff users belong to a reserved active system
    organization account so Spatie Teams always has a non-null
    account_id assignment context.

  Money       : decimal(15,2) unless more precision is required
  Quantities  : decimal(15,3)

4. SPATIE LARAVEL PERMISSION — TEAMS MODE
=====================================================================

Spatie Laravel Permission manages every live role and permission
assignment.

Configuration requirement (config/permission.php):

  'teams' => true,
  'column_names' => [
      'team_foreign_key'     => 'account_id',
      'role_pivot_key'       => null,
      'permission_pivot_key' => null,
      'model_morph_key'      => 'model_id',
  ],

The application must set the current account before checking roles
or permissions:

  app(\Spatie\Permission\PermissionRegistrar::class)
      ->setPermissionsTeamId($accountId);

The User model uses:

  use \Spatie\Permission\Traits\HasRoles;

Do not create a second custom live role/permission system alongside
Spatie Teams.

Foreign Keys:

  created_by_user_id -> users.id ON DELETE SET NULL

Role scopes:
  account_id = NULL               Global role available across accounts.
  account_id = current account id Custom role owned by that account.

Recommended global roles:

  Platform:
    super_admin
    admin
    admin_staff
    moderator
    support_agent
    finance_staff

  Account:
    primary_owner
    co_owner
    account_admin
    buyer_manager
    procurement_manager
    supplier_manager
    product_manager
    quotation_manager
    finance_manager

Rules:
  - Global standard roles are maintained by Platform Admin.
  - Account owners cannot edit or delete global roles.
  - An account owner may create account-specific custom roles.
  - To customize a global role, clone it into a role with
    account_id = current account id, then sync the clone's
    permissions.
  - Because MariaDB permits multiple NULL values in a unique key,
    the service layer must also enforce uniqueness of global role
    name + guard_name when account_id is NULL.

4.4 model_has_permissions
-------------------------
This table stores direct permissions assigned to a user in one
account context.

model_has_permissions
  account_id unsignedBigInteger FK accounts.id
  permission_id unsignedBigInteger FK permissions.id
  model_type varchar(255)
  model_id unsignedBigInteger

Primary Key:
  primary(account_id, permission_id, model_id, model_type)

Indexes:
  index(account_id)
  index(model_id, model_type)

Foreign Keys:
  account_id -> accounts.id ON DELETE CASCADE
  permission_id -> permissions.id ON DELETE CASCADE

Rules:
  - Use direct permissions only for exceptional per-user grants.
  - Prefer assigning permissions through roles.
  - Spatie does not provide an explicit deny pivot. Missing
    permission means denied.
  - Direct-permission assignment history is written to
    activity_log.


4.5 role_has_permissions
------------------------
role_has_permissions
  permission_id unsignedBigInteger FK permissions.id

Primary Key:
  primary(permission_id, role_id)

Foreign Keys:
  permission_id -> permissions.id ON DELETE CASCADE

  - The role itself carries account scope through
    roles.account_id; this pivot does not need account_id.


4.6 Reserved system account
---------------------------
Spatie Teams requires an account/team context for assignments.
Create one non-public organization account for Platform staff:

  accounts.account_number    = 'SYSTEM'
  accounts.account_type      = 'organization'
  accounts.display_name      = 'Edushopify Platform'
  accounts.is_system_account = true
  accounts.status            = 'active'

Rules:
  - The system account is not searchable or publicly visible.
  - Platform staff users are members of this account.
  - Platform roles are assigned in model_has_roles with this
    system account_id.
  - Normal Buyer/Supplier capabilities are not created for the
    system account.


4.7 Effective authorization order
---------------------------------
Every protected action must pass all applicable checks:

  1. User status is active.
  2. Account status is active.
  3. account_members status is active.
  4. Required Buyer/Supplier capability is active.
  5. Spatie team context is set to the current account_id.
  6. User has the required Spatie permission.
  7. The resource belongs to the current account.
  8. The resource status and workflow allow the action.

Default rule:
  No granted permission = deny.

Role names should not be used as the main business authorization
check. Use permissions plus Laravel Policies.

=====================================================================
8. SPATIE TEAM ROLE MANAGEMENT WORKFLOW
=====================================================================
This section defines runtime behavior only. It creates no
additional role/permission tables.

8.1 Standard roles
------------------
Platform Admin seeds global roles in roles with account_id = NULL
and attaches permissions through role_has_permissions.

Example:
  Role        : product_manager
  Scope       : supplier
  Permissions : listing.view
                listing.create
                listing.update
                listing.media.manage
                inventory.manage
                tier_pricing.manage

8.2 Organization custom roles
-----------------------------
An authorized owner may create an account-specific role:

  roles.account_id       = current account id
  roles.name             = 'junior_quotation_officer'
  roles.capability_scope = 'supplier'

The application then attaches selected global permissions through
role_has_permissions.

8.3 Customizing a standard role
-------------------------------
Global roles are immutable for normal organizations.

When an account wants different permissions:
  1. Select the global role.
  2. Clone it into a new role with roles.account_id = current
     account id.
  3. Copy current role permissions.
  4. Add or remove permissions on the cloned role.
  5. Assign the cloned role through model_has_roles.

This prevents one organization's changes from affecting other
organizations.

8.4 Assigning roles to members
------------------------------
After an invitation is accepted and an active account_members row
exists:
  1. Set the Spatie team context to account_members.account_id.
  2. Confirm the selected role is global or belongs to the same
     account.
  3. Confirm the role's capability scope is valid for the account.
  4. Assign the role with Spatie.
  5. Record the assignment in activity_log.

8.5 Direct permissions
----------------------
model_has_permissions may grant an exceptional permission directly
to a user.

Example:
  A Viewer temporarily receives quotation.view_received.

Direct permissions should be rare. If several members need the
same permission set, create a role.

8.6 No explicit permission denial
---------------------------------
This design does not use a custom deny table.

To remove an ability:
  - remove the permission from an account-specific role;
  - assign a different role; or
  - revoke a direct permission.

If a global role grants too much, clone it into an
account-specific role and remove the unwanted permission from the
clone.

8.7 Owner rules
---------------
account_members.member_type and is_primary_owner remain business
ownership fields.

The Spatie owner role controls application abilities, but it does
not replace ownership data.

Rules:
  - At least one active owner must remain.
  - The primary owner must transfer ownership before leaving.
  - Only the primary owner may close the account unless Platform
    Admin intervenes.
  - Owner-only permissions require both:
      - the Spatie permission; and
      - the required owner state in account_members.

8.8 Capability-aware role use
-----------------------------
A role assignment does not activate Buyer or Supplier business
access.

Example:
  User has product_manager role.
  Supplier capability = pending.
  Result: listing publication and RFQ access remain denied.

Both the Spatie permission and active account capability are
required.

30. IMPORTANT BACKEND AUTHORIZATION RULES
=====================================================================
These rules must not be implemented only in Vue, React, Blade, or
other frontend code.

30.1 Request context middleware
-------------------------------
For every authenticated account route:
  1. Resolve the authenticated user's active account_members row.
  2. Confirm user, account, and membership are active.
  3. Store current account_id and selected Buyer/Supplier
     capability in session.
  4. Validate the account_id in the route against the user's
     membership.
  5. Call setPermissionsTeamId(account_id).
  6. Clear/reload the user's role and permission relations after
     changing context.

The URL should also express capability context:
  /buyer/...
  /supplier/...
  /admin/...

30.2 Permission checks
----------------------
Use permissions rather than role names:
  $user->can('rfq.publish')
  $user->can('quotation.submit')
  $user->can('listing.create')

Roles are collections of permissions, not the final authorization
rule.

30.3 Full RFQ access
--------------------
A Supplier may open full RFQ details only when:
  user.status = active
  account.status = active
  account_members.status = active
  supplier capability.status = active
  subscription.status = active
  Spatie permission rfq_opportunity.view is granted
  rfq_supplier_queue.eligibility_status = eligible
  rfq_supplier_queue.available_at <= now
  rfq.status = open

For directly selected Suppliers:
  source = selected_supplier
  delay_minutes = 0

30.4 Submit quotation
---------------------
Require:
  active Supplier capability
  active subscription
  Spatie permission quotation.submit
  RFQ access
  RFQ open
  deadline not passed
  no accepted award
  quotation belongs to current Supplier account

30.5 Publish RFQ
----------------
Require:
  active Buyer capability
  account active
  buyer profile complete
  Spatie permission rfq.publish
  at least one RFQ item
  RFQ belongs to current Buyer account

30.6 Award quotation
--------------------
Require:
  active Buyer capability
  Spatie permission quotation.award
  Buyer account owns RFQ
  quotation belongs to RFQ
  quotation status is submitted/revised/shortlisted/under_review
  no active accepted award

30.7 Create Purchase Order
--------------------------
Require:
  award.status = accepted
  Spatie permission purchase_order.update_buyer or an authorized
  system workflow

30.8 Manage members and roles
-----------------------------
Require:
  members.invite / members.update / members.remove
  roles.create / roles.update / roles.delete / roles.assign

Additional rules:
  - The role being assigned must be global or belong to the same
    account.
  - The role capability scope must match an active or pending
    account capability as configured by the workflow.
  - Only Platform Admin can edit global roles and global
    permissions.
  - An organization cannot remove the last active owner.
  - An organization cannot assign Platform-scoped permissions.

30.9 Platform Admin
-------------------
Platform routes use the reserved system account team context.

Platform permissions include:
  platform.accounts.approve
  platform.capabilities.review
  platform.supplier_documents.verify
  platform.listings.moderate
  platform.tickets.manage

Do not assume that a role named admin automatically bypasses
domain rules. Use permissions and explicit Laravel Gates/Policies.
A super_admin Gate-before rule may be used only for authorized
platform-wide bypass behavior.

31. MAIN WORKFLOWS
=====================================================================

31.1 Registration
-----------------
 1. User registers.
 2. User chooses account type:
      - individual
      - organization
 3. User chooses initial capability:
      - Buyer
      - Supplier
      - Both
 4. System creates:
      - users
      - accounts
      - account_members
      - buyer_profiles and/or supplier_profiles
      - account_capabilities
 5. System sets Spatie team context to the new account_id.
 6. System assigns global primary_owner role through
    model_has_roles.
 7. User completes profile information.
 8. Capability application goes to Admin.
 9. Admin approves/rejects/requires revision.
10. Supplier selects a plan only after Supplier approval.

31.2 Add employee
-----------------
 1. Owner/member with members.invite creates an invitation.
 2. Owner may prefill employee name, email, phone, and proposed
    role.
 3. Employee receives a secure email.
 4. Employee creates a password and verifies email.
 5. System creates users and account_members rows.
 6. System sets the Spatie team context to the organization's
    account_id.
 7. System assigns the selected global or account-specific role.
 8. Role assignment is written to activity_log.
 9. User remains tied to this one account.

31.3 Create a custom role
-------------------------
 1. Owner has roles.create permission.
 2. Owner selects capability scope and permissions.
 3. System rejects Platform-scoped or owner-only permissions the
    owner may not delegate.
 4. System creates roles row with account_id = current account id.
 5. System syncs permissions through role_has_permissions.
 6. Role creation is written to activity_log.

31.4 Customize a standard role
------------------------------
 1. Owner selects a global role.
 2. System clones it to roles.account_id = current account id.
 3. System copies role_has_permissions.
 4. Owner modifies permissions on the clone.
 5. Members are assigned the account-specific clone.

31.5 Individual-to-organization conversion
------------------------------------------
 1. Individual submits proposed organization information.
 2. Existing personal account remains active.
 3. Admin reviews conversion.
 4. If rejected:
      - account remains individual
      - resubmission is allowed
 5. If approved:
      - same account_id remains
      - account_type becomes organization
      - change history is stored
      - Supplier capability becomes pending if it was active
      - organization rollback is prohibited
      - member-management permissions become usable according to
        assigned Spatie roles

31.6 Supplier onboarding
------------------------
 1. Supplier capability is approved by Admin.
 2. Supplier selects free or paid plan.
 3. Subscription becomes active.
 4. Supplier users still require the relevant Spatie permissions.
 5. Supplier may publish approved listings.
 6. Supplier receives eligible RFQs based on queue/delay.

31.7 Global RFQ
---------------
 1. Buyer with rfq.create creates RFQ and items.
 2. Buyer with rfq.publish selects global visibility and
    publishes.
 3. System creates queue rows for Supplier accounts.
 4. Backend calculates eligibility and available_at.
 5. Public summary is searchable.
 6. Eligible Suppliers with rfq_opportunity.view see full RFQ
    after delay.
 7. Interested Suppliers with quotation.submit submit quotations.

31.8 Selected-Supplier RFQ
--------------------------
 1. Buyer with rfq.invite_suppliers chooses one or multiple
    Suppliers.
 2. System creates rfq_selected_suppliers rows.
 3. Queue rows use source = selected_supplier.
 4. Delay = 0.
 5. Eligible selected Suppliers receive immediate access.

31.9 RFQ edit after quotations
------------------------------
 1. Authorized Buyer edits RFQ.
 2. System increments current_version_no.
 3. System creates rfq_change_logs row.
 4. Suppliers with submitted quotations are notified.
 5. Existing quotation stores old rfq_version_no.
 6. Supplier with quotation.revise may submit a revision for the
    new version.

31.10 Award and Purchase Order
------------------------------
 1. Buyer with quotation.compare compares quotations.
 2. Buyer with quotation.shortlist shortlists quotations.
 3. Buyer with quotation.award creates award attempt 1.
 4. Supplier with award.accept or award.reject responds before
    deadline.
 5. If rejected, Buyer may create another award attempt.
 6. If accepted:
      - RFQ becomes awarded
      - Purchase Order is created
 7. Purchase is fulfilled outside platform payment.
 8. Status progresses to completed.

  Admin creates subscription plans.

  Conversation history remains stored.

Inventory:
  EduShopify is a marketplace only.

  No warehouse inventory management in Phase 1.

Purchase Order:
  Phase 1 workflow: Award Accepted -> PO Issued -> Completed
  Manually.

Location data:
  Country, State, City data will be imported through Admin CSV.

37. DBA AND IMPLEMENTATION RECOMMENDATIONS
=====================================================================

 1. Do not rely on frontend checks for Spatie permissions,
    subscription, RFQ access, ownership, or capability status.
 2. Enable Spatie Teams before running the package migration and
    configure account_id as the team key.
 3. Add HasRoles only to the User model; do not create conflicting
    role, roles, permission, or permissions fields/relations on
    that model.
 4. Set Spatie's current team/account context in middleware before
    route model binding and permission checks.
 5. After changing account context, clear or reload the user's
    role and permission relations.
 6. Use permission checks rather than role-name checks for
    business actions.
 7. Use Laravel Policies/Gates and service-layer authorization
    after Spatie permission checks.
 8. Keep account_members even though Spatie Teams is enabled; it
    stores business membership and ownership state that Spatie
    does not manage.
 9. Keep account_capabilities; Buyer/Supplier approval is a
    business workflow, not a role assignment.
10. Use global roles for Platform-defined defaults and
    account-specific roles for organization customizations.
11. Never let an organization modify a global role. Clone it into
    an account-specific role instead.
12. Prefer role permissions over direct user permissions.
13. Use activity_log to audit role creation, role assignment,
    direct permission grants, permission synchronization, and
    revocation.
14. Use Spatie's API methods such as assignRole, syncRoles,
    givePermissionTo, and syncPermissions instead of writing
    directly to pivots.
15. Reset Spatie's cache after database seeding or any direct
    maintenance operation.
16. Wrap registration, employee acceptance, account conversion
    approval, award creation, and Purchase Order creation in
    database transactions.
17. Use queued jobs for RFQ queue creation, delayed notifications,
    rating recalculation, subscription expiry, RFQ expiry, and
    award response expiry.
18. Use immutable history tables for capability applications,
    account conversion, RFQ edits, quotation revisions, and
    Purchase Order status.
19. Do not allow physical deletion of accounts, RFQs, quotations,
    awards, Purchase Orders, reviews, or subscription payments
    after business activity exists.
20. Use application validation plus database constraints for all
    state transitions.
21. Add audit events for all Admin approvals and rejections.
22. Keep public RFQ summary data separate from full RFQ
    authorization.
23. Use indexed account foreign keys throughout the schema.
24. Test authorization with at least these cases:
      - same permission in two different accounts;
      - global role assigned in an account;
      - account-specific custom role;
      - suspended membership;
      - pending Supplier capability with Supplier role;
      - direct permission grant;
      - system account Platform role;
      - resource belonging to another account.


=====================================================================
38. FINAL ARCHITECTURE STATEMENT
=====================================================================
Edushopify V4.3 uses a stable account_id as both the business
identity and the Spatie Teams authorization context.

  User                  = human actor
  Account               = individual, organization, or reserved
                          internal platform organization
  Capability            = Admin-approved Buyer and/or Supplier
                          eligibility
  Profile               = capability-specific information
  Membership            = business relationship between User and
                          Account
  Spatie Role           = named permission collection, global or
                          account-specific
  Spatie Permission     = granular application ability
  Laravel Policy/Service= ownership, status, deadline,
                          subscription, and workflow validation

An individual may convert to an organization without transferring
business records because the same account_id remains. The
conversion is Admin-approved, audited, and irreversible.

Buyer and Supplier capabilities remain independent of Spatie
roles. A user may hold Supplier permissions, but Supplier actions
remain blocked until the account's Supplier capability is active
and other business conditions pass.

All live role and permission assignments are stored only in
Spatie's five tables:

  permissions
  roles
  model_has_roles
  model_has_permissions
  role_has_permissions

No parallel custom runtime role/permission system is used.
