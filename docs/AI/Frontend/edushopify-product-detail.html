Product Description

Lightweight, stackable, and easily reconfigurable modular desks for modern flexible classrooms. Rounded edges for safety, available in 6 colors, with casters option for quick room transitions. The Flex series is designed for K-12 environments and higher education settings requiring adaptive layouts for collaborative and individual learning modes.

Each desk features a scratch-resistant MDF surface, powder-coated steel frame, and non-slip feet. Optional caster wheels allow reconfiguration without lifting. Compatible with Flex Chair accessory range for a complete classroom furniture solution.
